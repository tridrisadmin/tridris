# Your developer view

Your private link opens a view of **your agent only**. Nobody else's agents, actions or settings appear there, and it holds no Tridris code.

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for your approval** | Actions your agent asked for that need a person. Open one to approve or reject it. |
| **Activity** | Every action your agent sent, newest first, with its status and the reason in plain words. Updates every few seconds. |
| **An action** | The timeline (requested, held, approved, executed), who approved it and how, and each record with **Verify**. |
| **Connect your agent** | The API address and a ready-to-run curl command. |
| **Approve on your phone** | Optional. Set up a passkey: approving then needs your phone's Face ID, fingerprint or PIN, and the signature is kept with the record. |
| **Policy** | Your agent's limits: which actions it may take, amounts, approval thresholds, hourly and daily limits. Saving creates a new version; new requests use it right away. |
| **Sample store** | Made-up customers and orders to act on, with buttons to copy their IDs. |

## Good to know

- **Keep the link private.** Anyone with it can approve your agent's actions and change its limits.
- **Approvals are recorded under your name**, with "button" or "passkey" as the method.
- **We reset the demo from time to time.** Your token and link keep working, your limits stay, past actions are cleared, and sample IDs change.
- **If the link stops working**, your access may have been turned off. Email info@tridris.com.
- Use made-up details only. Never send real customer data, card or bank details.
